ShiftMoon Support

A little help, when you need it.

Questions about shifts, pay or your account? Get in touch.

Email support

support@shiftmoon.app

Include your iOS version, app version and a short description. A screenshot helps — remove personal information first.

Subscriptions & billing

Apple subscriptions ↗

Manage or cancel in your Apple Account. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account deletion

Profile → ShiftMoon Cloud → Delete Account removes the cloud account and active workspace. Erase local data separately. Apple-linked accounts may require fresh sign-in. An Apple subscription is not cancelled automatically.

Login codes & sync

Check your inbox and spam folder, and use the latest code. Never send a code to support. For sync, open the app online and check that both devices use the same account.

Do not send passwords, login codes or unredacted payslips. If you cannot access an account, email from its address so we can verify ownership.
