A few questions,
before you start.

Is ShiftMoon free?

Yes. The calendar supports unlimited shifts. Work hours, gross pay estimates, the Today widget and optional Cloud Sync are free. Premium adds deeper pay tools, sleep planning, shift alarms and more widgets.

Does it work without an account?

Yes. You can keep your schedule on your iPhone and use it offline. An account is only needed if you choose Cloud Sync.

Can I use it for rotating or night shifts?

Yes. Plan morning, evening and overnight shifts with manual entries, reusable templates or a repeating rotation.

Are the earnings my exact salary?

They are estimates based on your entries and pay settings. Your employer’s records and actual payslip remain authoritative.

What does Cloud Sync cost?

Cloud Sync is free and optional. Apple, Google and email are ways to sign in to the same ShiftMoon Cloud service.

Is there an Android app?

The current release is for iPhone. An Android version is planned, but there is no release date or download yet.
